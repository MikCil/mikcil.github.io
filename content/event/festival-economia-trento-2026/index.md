"Un futuro perfettamente prevedibile" (*A perfectly predictable future*) was one of the panels of the 2026 Festival dell'Economia di Trento, whose theme this year was *Dal mercato ai nuovi poteri. Le speranze dei giovani* ("From the market to the new powers: the hopes of young people"). I spoke alongside Luca De Biase, journalist at Il Sole 24 Ore, and Ersilia Vaudo, Senior Executive at the European Space Agency.

My contribution was about how NLP and the humanities can work together, and why the tech industry needs people who are trained in both. The panel was in Italian, and the festival page lists it as available on demand.

More photos and a few notes are in [this post](/post/festival-economia-trento-2026/).
